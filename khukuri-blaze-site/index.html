01 · The Club

Passion, teamwork and a shared love of the game.

Khukuri Blaze Cricket Club is built on passion, teamwork and a shared love of cricket. We compete with pride, back every player's growth, and build friendships that outlast the season. Together we play with purpose and celebrate every achievement.

The club was founded in Canberra in 2025 by Yunesh Singh Thakuri, Kishor Mahato, Bishal Pachhai, Aashish Kumar and Asim Subedi, who gave it a crest of its own, a set of colours, and a weekend worth turning up for. The khukuri we carry stands for work and for the people who came before us; the flame is what we ask of each other on the field.

We are a Nepalese community club with the door open to everyone. Experienced grade cricketers, players finding their way back to the game, and people who have never held a bat all train in the same nets. Everyone is welcome at Khukuri Blaze Cricket Club.

How we got here

2025

The club is formedKhukuri Blaze is founded in Canberra - crest, colours, and the motto that stuck: unleash the fire.

25/26

First full seasonEighteen matches across roughly six months of CCSCA red ball cricket. Ten wins, fifth on the ladder, and five straight victories heading into Round 17 - the semifinals missed by a whisker.

2026

Our own tournamentAfter the CCSCA season and a short T10 competition, the club organised and hosted the KTM Friendship Cup, Edition 1 - thirteen matches across four clubs.

Aug 26

Beyond the boundary

Looking after our own.

When flooding hit Nepal, players, families and supporters gave what they could. The club collected NPR 25,000 and sent it to the Prime Minister's Disaster Relief Fund to help people recovering from the floods.

It is not a large sum, but it was given together, and we will do the same whenever our community back home needs a hand.
